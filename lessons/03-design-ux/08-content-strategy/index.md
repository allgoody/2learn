## What it is

Content strategy decides what content exists, for whom, in what voice, and how it is maintained after launch. It covers the pages and strings a product ships, the help and policy text behind them, and the rules that keep them consistent when several people write them.

## Why it matters

Content is the part of a product nobody owns, so it rots. Screens are redesigned around copy written for the previous structure; help pages describe two releases ago; the same concept appears under three names. The cost shows up as support calls and as quiet abandonment. A written strategy is small — a page of rules and an owner — and it is the difference between content that is maintained and content that accumulates.
