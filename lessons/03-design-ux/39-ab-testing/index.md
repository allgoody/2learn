## What it is

A/B testing shows two variants to comparable groups and lets behaviour decide between them. One variable changes; everything else stays the same; the difference in the outcome is attributed to that variable. It is the only way to answer "does this change help?" with evidence rather than opinion.

## Why it matters

Most design changes are shipped with a story about why they will help, and a fair number of those stories are wrong. A test converts the story into a number, and — more usefully — it settles the argument about which of two reasonable options is better without anyone losing face. It also creates a habit of measuring after release, which is where most of the learning about real users comes from.
