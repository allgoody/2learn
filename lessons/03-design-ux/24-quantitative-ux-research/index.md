## What it is

Quantitative UX research uses numbers to describe behaviour at the scale interviews cannot reach: how many people start, where they stop, how long a task takes and how often it succeeds. It describes what happened; it does not explain why. The difference from A/B Testing is deliberate — this is measuring, that is intervening.

## Why it matters

Five interviews tell you a problem exists; a funnel tells you whether it costs 2% or 40% of your users, which decides whether it is next week's work or next year's. Without numbers, prioritisation is decided by whoever tells the most vivid story, and the most vivid story is usually the most recent one rather than the most common. The numbers also survive the departure of the person who ran the interviews.
