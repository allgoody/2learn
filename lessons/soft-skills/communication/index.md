## What it is

Communication at work is getting an idea, a decision or a problem into someone else's head accurately enough that they can act on it: explaining to people with different context, disagreeing without stalling, and writing so the reader does not have to ask. It is a skill with techniques, not a personality trait.

## Why it matters

Most "technical" delays are communication delays: a decision that was never clearly made, a risk nobody heard, a design that was understood differently by three people in the room. The cost is invisible because it looks like engineering time. It also compounds — the person who cannot explain their work does not get their work adopted, and the team that cannot disagree productively either avoids hard problems or argues about them in a meeting instead of solving them.
