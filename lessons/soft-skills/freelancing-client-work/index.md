## What it is

Freelancing is doing the work and running the business around it: finding clients, agreeing what is being delivered and for how much, and getting paid for it. The artefacts are a proposal, a scope with a definition of done, and a contract with a payment schedule.

## Why it matters

The technical work is the part most freelancers already have. What fails is everything else, and it fails expensively: a scope that grows because nobody wrote down what was included, a price set before the work was understood, an invoice that arrives after the client's budget year closed. These are not personality problems or bad luck; they are ordinary risks with known mitigations, and the mitigations are the difference between a business and an expensive hobby.
