## What it is

Stakeholder management is keeping the people who can fund, block or reshape the work aligned and informed. The artefact is a stakeholder map — who they are, how much power and interest they have, what they need and when — plus a communication plan that says what each of them hears and how often.

## Why it matters

Projects rarely fail on the technical work and often fail on an unconsulted person with the authority to stop them. The finance lead who first hears about the change when the invoice fails, the support manager whose team absorbs the extra calls, the compliance officer whose sign-off nobody asked for. Naming them early makes the objections arrive while they are still cheap to act on, and it turns the escalation path from an ambush into a scheduled conversation.
