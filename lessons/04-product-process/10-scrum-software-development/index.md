## What it is

Scrum is the most common agile framework: fixed-length sprints, three defined accountabilities, four ceremonies and a backlog that is refined continuously. It is one way to implement the values in Agile Methodology, and it is a specific one — it prescribes who decides what, and when.

## Why it matters

Scrum's value is that it makes the iteration explicit and gives a team a schedule they do not have to renegotiate: the sprint boundary is fixed, so the question each day is only what to finish before it. Its failure mode is equally specific and worth stating plainly: Scrum becomes a reporting ritual. When the daily stand-up exists to update a manager, when the retrospective produces no change, and when the sprint is treated as a commitment negotiated under pressure, the framework is delivering its meetings and none of its benefits. Scrum suits work that can be planned in a short batch; it is a poor fit when the work arrives unpredictably and cannot wait for the boundary.
