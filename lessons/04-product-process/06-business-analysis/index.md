## What it is

Business analysis maps how an organisation works today — its processes, the people involved, the systems and the gaps between them — before any solution is chosen. Its artefacts are a process map, a stakeholder list and a gap analysis. It answers "what is actually happening, and what does it cost?" rather than "what should we build?".

## Why it matters

Solutions proposed without this map routinely solve the wrong layer. A team automates a step that exists only because an approval happens to sit in the wrong department; a system is replaced when a policy change would have removed the need for it. The map also exposes who is affected, which decides whether a change is feasible at all. Its failure mode is analysis that never stops: a diagram that takes a quarter to produce and is out of date when the solution starts. Time-box the mapping to the decision it must inform.
