## What it is

A journey map charts every step, touchpoint and emotion across an end-to-end experience — from the moment someone realises they have a need to the moment it is met, and often the aftermath. It is drawn from research, spans channels, and shows not only what happened but how it felt.

## Why it matters

Teams optimise the screen they own, which is why the worst part of most experiences is the seam between two screens. A journey map makes the seams visible by forcing the whole sequence onto one page: the search, the phone call, the wait, the email, the return visit. It also reframes "the user" as a person with a timeline, which changes what teams argue about — from which feature is best to which moment matters most.
