## What it is

A prototype is a clickable or coded draft good enough to put in front of real users. It is not a specification and not a demo: its only job is to answer a question that a wireframe or a conversation cannot. Fidelity is chosen from the question, not from the tool or the team's mood.

## Why it matters

Arguments about wording, order and behaviour are settled in minutes by watching someone attempt the task and in hours by discussion. A prototype is the cheapest artefact that produces that evidence. It also exposes what a static layout hides — a missing state, a dead end, a flow that takes seven screens — while the cost of change is still a redraw rather than a rewrite.
