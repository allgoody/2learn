## What it is

A user interview is a one-to-one conversation that surfaces goals, mental models and pain points. It is not a usability test — nothing is shown and nothing is attempted — and it is not a survey read aloud. The participant talks about their own experience in their own words, and you write down what they did.

## Why it matters

Interviews are the cheapest way to learn why people do what the analytics show, and the easiest to do badly. A leading question, a participant who answers about the future, or a researcher who explains the product all produce confident fiction. Done well, six interviews reveal the vocabulary, the sequence and the workarounds that shape everything you design next — and give you the exact quotes that convince a sceptical stakeholder.
