## What it is

Usability testing is watching people attempt tasks on your design and recording where it breaks. It is an activity, distinct from usability, which is the quality the design has. The sessions are short, the participants are few, and the output is a ranked list of findings rather than a score.

## Why it matters

Everything else in this topic produces opinions about a design; a usability test produces an observation. That difference settles arguments: the debate about whether a label is clear ends the first time two participants misread it. It is also cheap at the right moment — five people, one afternoon, no working code — and its findings are the most actionable in design, because they name a specific element and a specific failure.
