## What it is

Data visualization turns numbers into charts a non-expert reads correctly. That last word is the whole job: a chart that is accurate but misread has failed. It covers choosing the form, scaling the axes honestly, ordering the marks and labelling enough that no explanation is needed.

## Why it matters

A chart is a claim about the data, and most charts in products make a claim their author would not defend if asked. Truncated axes exaggerate small differences, area encodes a value it does not represent, and colour gradients imply an order that is not there. Readers check none of this: they take the shape of the chart as the finding. That is a design responsibility with a number attached to it.
