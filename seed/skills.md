## Overlaps worth knowing

- **UI/UX naming** — "UI/UX Design", "UI", "UX" and "User Experience Design" are four wordings of one field. They are umbrella entries in [design-ux.md](design-ux.md), not four skills.
- **HTML and CSS** — an umbrella for the two entries either side of it in [frontend.md](frontend.md).
- **Cloud umbrellas** — "Cloud Computing", "Cloud Development", "Cloud Applications", "Cloud Deployment" and "Application Deployment" mostly restate each other; [cloud-devops.md](cloud-devops.md) marks them as such.
- **Not overlapping, despite looking it** — Unit / Integration / End-to-End testing are genuinely three different skills. Same for "Usability" (a quality) versus "Usability Testing" (an activity).
