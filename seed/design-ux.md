# Design & UX

Product and experience design: research, structure, interface, evaluation.

Part of the [skills index](skills.md). Start here: **Interaction Design**, **User Research**, **Usability Testing**.

## Foundations & process

- **Design Thinking** — Human-centred problem-solving loop: empathise, define, ideate, prototype, test.
- **User Centered Design** — Designing from the user's tasks and constraints, not from the org chart or the tech.
- **Lean UX** — Small, fast build-measure-learn cycles instead of a long upfront specification.
- **Jobs-to-be-Done** — Framing a need as the job the user hires the product to do.
- **Service Design** — Designing the whole service journey, including staff, channels and backstage.
- **Facilitation & Workshop Design** — Running a structured session that gets a group to a decision.
- **Storyboarding** — Sketching a user's sequence of steps as a comic so the flow can be agreed on.
- **Sketching** — Fast, low-fidelity drawing to think ideas through before committing to a tool.

## Research

- **User Research** — Finding out what people actually need before designing anything.
- **UI/UX Research** — The same, narrowed to the interface: what works, what confuses, what is missing.
- **Design Research** — Studying users, context and existing products to frame the design problem.
- **User Interviews** — One-to-one conversations that surface goals, mental models and pain points.
- **Survey Design** — Writing neutral questions that measure what you think they measure.
- **Card Sorting** — Users group topics themselves, revealing their mental model for menus and categories.
- **Journey Mapping** — Charting every step, touchpoint and emotion across an end-to-end experience.
- **Persona (User Experience)** — A named, evidence-based profile of a user group to design against.
- **Quantitative UX Research** — Numbers at scale: funnels, drop-off, task success, time on task.
- **A/B Testing** — Shipping two variants and letting behaviour decide which one wins.
- **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** — The standard web analytics tool: events, funnels, acquisition reports.

## Content & writing

- **UX Writing & Microcopy** — The words in buttons, empty states and errors — usually where users get stuck.
- **Content Strategy** — Deciding what content exists, for whom, in what voice, and how it is maintained.

## Structure & interaction

- **Information Architecture** — Organising and labelling content so people can find it.
- **Wireframing** — Grey-box layouts that fix structure and hierarchy before any visual polish.
- **Prototyping** — Clickable or coded drafts good enough to put in front of real users.
- **Interaction Design** — Designing moment-to-moment behaviour: states, feedback, flow.

## Visual design

- **Visual Design** — Hierarchy, spacing, contrast and alignment — making an interface look intentional.
- **Typography** — Choosing and setting type: scale, line length, rhythm, readability.
- **Color Theory** — Contrast, harmony and meaning; how colour reads in context and for colour-blind users.
- **Layout & Grid Systems** — Columns, gutters and breakpoints that make screens predictable.
- **Data Visualization** — Turning numbers into charts a non-expert can read correctly.

## Design systems

- **Design Systems** — A shared, documented library of components and rules so teams stay consistent.
- **Design Tokens** — Named values (colour, spacing, type) shared between design tools and code.
- **Design Handoff & Specs** — Packaging intent — states, edge cases, tokens — so engineers build it right.

## Evaluation

- **Usability** — How easily a real user reaches their goal without error or confusion.
- **Usability Testing** — Watching people attempt tasks on your design, and recording where it breaks.
- **Heuristic Evaluation** — Expert review against established usability rules, to catch obvious faults fast.

## Tools

- **Figma (Design Software)** — The industry-standard collaborative UI design and prototyping tool.
- **Adobe XD** — Adobe's UI design and prototyping tool; common where Adobe is already the standard.
- **Framer** — Design tool that publishes interactive, near-production prototypes and sites.
- **Miro** — Infinite whiteboard for workshops, journey maps and affinity diagrams.
- **Maze** — Unmoderated usability and prototype testing with automatic metrics.
- **Notion** — Docs and lightweight project/wiki space used to hold research and specs.
- **Hotjar** — Session recordings, heatmaps and on-site surveys on real traffic.

## Umbrella terms

Original-list entries that name the field rather than one skill; they map onto everything above.

- **User Interface and User Experience (UI/UX) Design** — The whole discipline; not learnable as a single skill.
- **User Interface (UI)** — The visual and interactive surface; covered by the visual and interaction sections.
- **User Experience** — The overall felt quality of using something; covered by research and evaluation.
- **User Experience Design** — The same discipline as UI/UX design, worded the other way round.
