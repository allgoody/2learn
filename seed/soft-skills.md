# Soft skills

Part of the [skills index](skills.md).

## Working with people

- **Communication** — Explaining clearly to technical and non-technical people alike.
- **Teamwork & Collaboration** — Sharing context, unblocking others, disagreeing without stalling.
- **Mentoring** — Reviewing, pairing and growing someone else's skill.

## Getting things done

- **Problem Solving** — Breaking an unclear problem down until it is actionable.
- **Time Management** — Estimating, prioritising and protecting focus time.

## Career

- **Technical Presentation** — Presenting work and decisions so a room can follow and decide.
- **Portfolio Building** — Case studies that show the thinking, not just the screenshots.
- **Interview Prep & Whiteboarding** — Practice under observation: coding, design and system questions.
- **Freelancing & Client Work** — Scoping, pricing, contracts and clients who actually pay.
