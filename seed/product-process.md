# Product & process

How work is chosen, scoped and delivered.

Part of the [skills index](skills.md). Start here: **Agile Methodology**, **Requirements Engineering**, **Prioritization Frameworks (RICE / MoSCoW)**.

## Agile & delivery

- **Agile Methodology** — Short iterations, working software over documentation, respond to change.
- **Agile Software Development** — The same values applied concretely by an engineering team.
- **Scrum (Software Development)** — The most common Agile framework: sprints, roles, ceremonies, backlog.
- **Kanban** — Flow-based board with WIP limits; fewer meetings than Scrum.
- **User Story** — A short need statement ("As a… I want… so that…") used to plan work.
- **Estimation & Story Points** — Sizing work relatively rather than in hours, and why that helps.
- **Jira & Confluence** — The standard tracker and wiki pair in Agile shops.

## Product

- **Product Management** — Deciding what to build, why, and in what order, against a business goal.
- **Product Discovery** — Research and experiments to find a solution worth building at all.
- **Roadmapping** — Sequencing outcomes and themes over time, not a fixed feature list.
- **Prioritization Frameworks (RICE / MoSCoW)** — Turning a wish list into an argued order.
- **Requirements Engineering** — Capturing what a system must do, testably and unambiguously.
- **Business Analysis** — Mapping processes, stakeholders and gaps before a solution is chosen.
- **Stakeholder Management** — Keeping the people who can block or fund the work aligned and informed.
- **User Acceptance Testing** — The client confirming the built thing meets the agreed need.
- **Technical Writing** — Documentation, specs and READMEs a stranger can follow.

## Umbrella terms

- **Software Development** — Umbrella; the whole discipline of building software.
- **Application Development** — Umbrella; the same thing viewed from the application's side.
- **Software Development Life Cycle** — Umbrella; the phases from requirements through maintenance.
